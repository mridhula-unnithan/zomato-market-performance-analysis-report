# 📊 Zomato Market & Performance Analysis

An end-to-end exploratory data analysis (EDA) and business intelligence project evaluating how operational features, pricing structures, and customer engagement influence restaurant satisfaction and market positioning across 141 cities.

---

## 📌 Executive Summary

This study analyzes **9,551 restaurant listings** sourced from Zomato to uncover key drivers of customer ratings, revenue potential, and market saturation. By combining statistical correlation analysis with strategic market segmentation, the report translates raw data into actionable recommendations for restaurant operators, investors, and platform managers.

---

## 🎯 Key Analytical Findings

* **The Online Delivery Premium (+0.78 Rating Bump):** Restaurants offering online delivery achieve an average aggregate rating of **3.25 / 5.0**, compared to **2.47 / 5.0** for dine-in-only establishments.
* **Price Tier Performance Split:** Customer satisfaction scales directly with price range. High-end restaurants (**Price Range 4**) average **3.82 / 5.0**, whereas budget options (**Price Range 1**) average **2.00 / 5.0**, highlighting operational and quality consistency issues in low-cost dining.
* **Market Concentration:** North Indian (**3,960 listings**) and Chinese (**2,735 listings**) dominate market volume, creating high saturation compared to underrepresented categories like Italian, Continental, and specialized Cafes.
* **Key Drivers of Rating:** Linear correlation reveals that **Price Range ($r = 0.44$)** and **Total Vote Count ($r = 0.31$)** are the strongest positive linear predictors of higher customer satisfaction scores.

---

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations

1. **Mandate Online Delivery:** Onboarding delivery capabilities serves as the single most effective operational lever to improve visibility and boost aggregate customer ratings.
2. **Quality Control for Budget Dining:** Operators in Price Range 1 must standardize service quality to bridge the **1.82-point rating gap** against premium establishments.
3. **Target Niche Cuisine Investments:** Investors should target underserved, high-margin categories (Cafes, Bakeries, Italian) to avoid head-to-head competition in saturated comfort food segments.
4. **Drive Review Volume:** Leveraging digital prompts or loyalty rewards to increase vote volume builds social proof and strongly correlates with higher rating performance.
